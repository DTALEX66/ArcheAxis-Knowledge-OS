## 输入状态机

```text
raw
  ↓
quarantined
  ↓
sanitized
  ↓
candidate
  ↓
approved
  ↓
active
```

异常状态：

```text
blocked
rejected
archived
conflicted
```

## B线运行状态机

```text
ingested
  ↓
routed
  ↓
context_built
  ↓
task_compiled
  ↓
permission_checked
  ↓
executed
  ↓
traced
  ↓
evaluated
  ↓
lesson_created
```

## 关键规则

```text
外部内容不直接进入 active
AI 输出不直接进入 active
TaskPack 不等于自动执行
MachineLesson 必须可追溯到 ExecutionTrace
高风险任务只进入 REVIEW / blocked
```
